Driving the Change

Hydrogen Fuel Racing is a student-led team at the University of Glasgow, building hydrogen-powered vehicles and creating hands-on opportunities across engineering, business, media and outreach.

We are composed of divisions across land, sea and air, all working towards cleaner transport and practical experience for students.
