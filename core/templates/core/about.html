About HFR

Who We Are

Glasgow's student-led hydrogen fuel racing team, engineering the zero-emission vehicles of tomorrow, competing on the world stage today.

Our Mission

Racing Towards a Hydrogen Future

HFR was founded to give students with no prior experience, but genuine passion for engineering, the chance to work on unique projects and innovate in the sustainable and clean energy sector.

We develop hydrogen fuel cell vehicles for competition across land, sea, and air, proving that performance and sustainability aren't opposites; they're the same destination.

Along the way, members gain real-world engineering, business, and creative experience that sets them apart in industry.

HFR CONSTITUTION

  1. Hydrogen Fuel Racing is a society welcome to all regardless of background and experience.
  2. Hydrogen Fuel Racing supports the race to net-zero by remaining sustainability centric.
  3. Hydrogen Fuel Racing must give back to the community. HFR must conduct STEM outreach and engage with the next generation.
  4. The success of one division is the success for all divisions. A loss for one division is a loss for all. Divisions must stay united throughout the ups and downs.
  5. Hydrogen Fuel Racing members must work hard to achieve the highest level of engineering. The society must work to produce the best engineers from the University.
  6. Hydrogen Fuel Racing must ensure an inclusive and engaging atmosphere.
Muhemmed Bin Naseeb
